# Decision Log

Record meaningful changes in audience, problem, offer, pricing, channel, build scope, or whether to continue. Keep entries short and link to the evidence that prompted them.

| Date | Decision | Evidence considered | Alternatives | Rationale | Next review / trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Workspace created; idea remains open | No idea evidence collected yet | N/A | Begin with problem discovery, not a presumed solution | After first customer conversations |

## Idea revisions

When the idea changes materially, copy the former one-sentence idea and audience here with the reason for changing it. Do not erase a useful failed hypothesis.

## Go / revise / stop history

For each decision, capture:

- What evidence existed at the time, including evidence against the idea.
- What was uncertain and what was not tested.
- The personal time and cash budget for the next step.
- What result would change the decision.
